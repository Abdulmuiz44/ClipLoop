## Style Prompt
Explosive, premium dark-tech motion language for ClipLane: high contrast black canvas, crisp white typography, electric cyan accents, sharp kinetic transitions, punchy scale and slide entrances, and controlled glow effects that feel product-grade rather than flashy noise.

## Colors
- `#050505` - base background
- `#FFFFFF` - primary text
- `#9CA3AF` - secondary text
- `#22D3EE` - accent glow and transition edge
- `#111827` - card surface

## Typography
- `Inter, system-ui, sans-serif`
- `Space Grotesk, Inter, system-ui, sans-serif`

## What NOT to Do
- Do not use pastel or warm palettes.
- Do not use soft, slow, floaty animations as the primary rhythm.
- Do not fill the screen with dense particle clutter.
- Do not use rounded bubbly UI shapes.
- Do not use low-contrast gray-on-gray text.
